# KISEKAE

> **Fashion, before it's yours.**

## Overview

Kisekae is an online fashion store that lets users try on clothing using augmented reality through their phone's camera, so they can make confident purchases.

## Features

### Authentication

- Sign up with email or phone number
- Log in with a password or an OTP sent to email
- Reset a forgotten password
- Change password
- JWT-based authentication

### Augmented Reality Try-On

- Point your phone camera at a subject to see how a clothing item looks in real time
- Alternatively, upload a picture of the subject from your gallery
- The outfit adjusts naturally as the subject moves, turns, or changes posture
- Capture a photo or short clip of yourself in the outfit to save or compare later

### Mix & Match

- Combine multiple products in the AR try-on and see how they fit together
- Switch between different sizes or colors of the same item while trying it on
- Save outfits as combos
- Share saved combos as a URL that others can open and try on

### Product Catalog

- Browse the clothing collection organized by categories
- Search with filters for size, color, price range, and clothing type
- Product cards with thumbnail image, available colors, and price
- Personalized, curated feed of recommended products

### Product Pages

- Detailed product view with images, description, price, and available sizes/colors
- Reviews and ratings
- Size chart to help pick the correct fit
- Direct access to launch AR try-on from the page

### Wishlist

- Save items for later
- Bulk-move wishlisted items directly to the cart

### User Profile

- Manage personal details, saved addresses, and payment methods
- View saved try-on snapshots linked to your profile
- View orders and check their status
- Change settings

### Cart & Checkout

- Add items to the cart, edit quantities, and select size/color before purchase
- Secure payment processing with multiple payment methods (cards, UPI, wallets)
- Order summary
- Estimated delivery time

### Notification System

- Order confirmation, shipping, and delivery updates via email and in-app push notifications
- Choose which channels (email / SMS / push) to receive notifications on, via profile settings
- Promotional and sales notifications

## Additional Features

### Advanced Clothing Physics

- Cloth drapes, stretches, and moves naturally with body motion rather than sitting rigid
- Materials such as silk and cotton behave distinctly
- Realistic lighting and shading that account for ambient light

### Shop the Look

- Search by uploading a screenshot or image
- Get suggestions for matching products and outfit combos

### Group Try-On

- Multiple users from different locations can form a group
- Try on various clothing live together
- Built-in video call

### AR-Based Size & Fit Check

- Suggests the appropriate size for the subject
- Checks whether the outfit properly fits the subject
